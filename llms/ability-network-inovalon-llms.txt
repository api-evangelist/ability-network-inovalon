# ABILITY Network (Inovalon)

> ABILITY Network is a healthcare SaaS business acquired by Inovalon in 2018, now delivered as part of the cloud-based Inovalon ONE Platform. It provides US healthcare providers with medical billing and claims management, eligibility verification, revenue cycle management, clinical quality reporting, and workforce/staffing and care coordination, backed by large national healthcare datasets and analytics.
